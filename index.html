⚖ Privacy and research ethics

🔒 Data handling

Scores are computed in your browser. The clicked coordinates and search radius are sent to public services to fetch data (Overpass/OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, Nominatim, map-tile servers) and, only if you enable them, to enrichment or language-model providers. API keys stay in this browser. Survey files are processed locally.

🌐 Open data

OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and CAMS/Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). No proprietary data are required for the NPPE method.

⚖ Interpretation

NPPE results are screening outputs to inform, not replace, planning judgement; they are not validated against ridership, expert or perception assessments.
